M&A Technical Advisory

Navigate technology aspects of mergers and acquisitions with expert guidance.

Strategic Technology Assessment for Transactions

Technology has become a critical factor in virtually every M&A transaction. Whether you're acquiring a software company, a technology-enabled business, or a traditional enterprise with significant IT infrastructure, understanding the technology landscape is essential to making informed decisions.

BCE Associates provides comprehensive M&A technical advisory services that help buyers, sellers, and investors understand the true state of technology assets, identify risks and opportunities, and plan for successful integration or carve-out.

Our M&A Advisory Services Include:

  • Pre-LOI Technology Assessment: Quick-turn evaluations to inform deal strategy before significant commitment
  • Comprehensive Technical Due Diligence: Deep-dive analysis of architecture, code, infrastructure, and team
  • Integration Planning: Technology roadmaps for combining systems and teams post-acquisition
  • Carve-Out Support: Technical planning for separating technology assets in divestitures
  • Synergy Validation: Realistic assessment of anticipated technology cost savings and revenue opportunities
  • Vendor Due Diligence: Seller-side technical preparation to streamline deal processes

Build Your Distributed Engineering Team

The global talent market offers tremendous opportunities for organizations willing to look beyond their immediate geography. Eastern Europe, in particular, has emerged as a powerhouse of technical talent, with strong educational institutions, deep engineering culture, and competitive cost structures.

BCE Associates helps organizations navigate the complexities of building and managing distributed engineering teams. Whether you need to augment your existing team, establish an offshore development center, or source contributors for open source projects, we provide end-to-end support.

  • Open Source Strategy: Building and managing teams for open source contributions alongside proprietary development
