Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from organizations considering our services.

How quickly can you start an engagement?

For urgent needs, we can often mobilize within 48-72 hours. Standard engagements typically begin within 1-2 weeks of agreement. We maintain dedicated capacity for time-sensitive M&A transactions.

Do you work with companies of all sizes?

Yes. We work with organizations ranging from funded startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Our engagement models and scope can be tailored to fit your organization's size and budget while still delivering meaningful value.

What industries do you specialize in?

While we serve clients across many industries, we have particularly deep experience in financial services, healthcare, SaaS/technology, e-commerce, and manufacturing. Our technical expertise is broadly applicable regardless of industry vertical.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Confidentiality is foundational to our business. All engagements are covered by comprehensive NDAs, and we maintain strict information barriers between clients. Our team members are trained in secure information handling, and we follow industry best practices for data protection.

Can you support global organizations?

Absolutely. Our distributed team operates across multiple time zones, and we have experience working with multinational organizations. We're comfortable conducting assessments remotely or on-site at locations worldwide.

What makes BCE Associates different from other advisory firms?

Our differentiation comes from three key factors: (1) our advisors are practitioners with hands-on technical experience, not just consultants; (2) our deep expertise in Eastern European talent markets; and (3) our security-first mindset that's woven into everything we do, from due diligence to team building.
